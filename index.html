Curriculum — 10 modules
MOD 01

Introduction & Interface

Getting familiar with the editing software layout, panels, and where everything lives.

MOD 02

Importing Media

Bringing in footage and photos from your phone or camera and organizing them before you start cutting.

MOD 03

Basic Editing

Cutting, trimming, and arranging clips into a rough sequence.

MOD 04

Audio Editing

Adjusting levels, cleaning up sound, and syncing audio to video.

MOD 05

Text & Titles

Adding captions, lower-thirds, and on-screen text that's easy to read.

MOD 06

Transitions

Using cuts and transitions to move smoothly between shots.

MOD 07

Effects & Filters

Applying color adjustments and filters to make footage look consistent.

MOD 08

Overlays & Animation

Adding simple graphics, logos, and animated elements on top of the footage.

MOD 09

Exporting

Choosing the right settings to export a finished video for social media or presentations.

MOD 10

Final Project

Putting it all together in one complete edited video from start to finish.
